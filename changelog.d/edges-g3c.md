- **The edge catalogue of `build-part` holds six classes that part G3c of the sandbox plan found
  late.** A swallowed cleanup error, a start-up `rmtree` of a stale directory that can hold the
  user's work, an `OSError` between two renames, a `finally` whose unlink replaces the first
  error, a recovery text that a routine prune makes false, and a timeout that kills a wrapper but
  not the program it started. `plan-parts` now writes these edges into a spec, and the round-1
  critic probes them.
