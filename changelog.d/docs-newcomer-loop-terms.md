- **The glossary defines the six terms of the builder-critic loop, and the newcomer check reads
  the entry pages of Agentic coding and Safeguards.** The glossary of the Introduction has entries
  for worktree, part, spec, mutant, critic and edge, each with a link to the page that treats it.
  The newcomer check reads a link to a glossary entry relative to the directory of its page, so
  `agentic/challenges.md` and `safeguards/risks.md` join its pages, and the first use of each term
  on them links its entry. "Part" has an entry but is not a term of the check, because the
  newcomer pages use it as the plain word. The risks table of *Risks of agentic coding* has a
  column *kind*, which says of each safeguard whether it is accident prevention or a boundary, and
  *Leak checks and gitleaks* links the section of *Private data across machines* that keeps the
  private values out of the sources.
