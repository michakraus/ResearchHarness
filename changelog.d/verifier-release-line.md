- **`julia-branch-verifier` keeps a finding the branch causes out of *Pre-existing, adjacent*, and reports the release bump on a `Release:` line.** A diff-caused finding goes under *Fixed* or *Unresolved*, so the section that says "not caused by this branch" is true. The bump that a rename or a signature change needs is not a defect: it reaches the PR body on its own line and does not stop the PR.
